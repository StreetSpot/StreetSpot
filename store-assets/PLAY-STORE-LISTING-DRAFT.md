## Full description
Discover what is happening around you with StreetSpot, a real-time local discovery and engagement platform connecting people with nearby food trucks, street vendors, pop-up shops, local businesses, events, and hidden gems.

FIND YOUR NEXT LOCAL FAVORITE
• Explore nearby food trucks, mobile vendors, pop-up shops, independent businesses, skate spots, community markets, and hidden gems.
• Discover local events and see where the action is happening.
• Save favorite places and use saved-spot tools to make it easier to return to places that matter to you.
• View business updates, deals, specials, and relocation announcements where available.
• Connect with businesses directly through available messaging and social features.

FOR VENDORS AND LOCAL BUSINESSES
• Manage listing details and live status where enabled for your account.
• Help nearby customers find your truck, cart, pop-up, or storefront.
• Share updates about specials, events, and location changes with your audience where supported.
• Access vendor tools for growth and business activity tracking where available.

HOST A PRIVATE EVENT
• Create invitations for weddings, graduations, birthdays, reunions, and other celebrations.
• Choose from Elegant, Celebration, and StreetSpot invitation styles.
• Share individual invitation links with selected guests.
• Keep private event venue details separate from the public discovery map.
• Review package details and guest limits before secure checkout.

MORE WAYS TO USE STREETSPOT
• Find flea markets, garage sales, pop-up tables, and neighborhood happenings.
• Save places you want to revisit and discover new community favorites.
• Use supported trip, parking, and travel tools to keep useful location information close at hand.

DISCOVER LOCAL. SUPPORT LOCAL.
StreetSpot brings local discoveries, community events, independent businesses, and private celebrations into one place. Available tools can vary by account, location, and app version.
